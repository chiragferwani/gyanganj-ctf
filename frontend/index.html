The Hidden City
Deep in the Himalayas, beyond maps and beyond time, lies Gyanganj — the mythical hidden city of immortal Siddhas, said to be the origin point of the Mahabharata itself. It was here that Maharishi Ved Vyasa first conceived the entire epic before a single word was written.

Lord Ganesha agreed to be his scribe on one condition: Vyasa must never pause while dictating, or Ganesha would stop writing forever. To keep pace, Vyasa wove riddles into the narration whenever he needed a moment to think.

Gyanganj is said to still hold the "unwritten verse" — a fragment of the Mahabharata that Vyasa composed but never dictated, sealed away because it contained knowledge too dangerous to be widely known. The immortal Siddhas of Gyanganj have guarded it ever since — refusing speech to all but the most worthy seekers, and even then, only in riddles.
